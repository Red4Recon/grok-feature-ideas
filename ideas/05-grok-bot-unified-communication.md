# 5. Grok Bot Unified Communication

**Bottom line: New revenue, $200M+ a year (Mike's earlier draft estimate). Headline: worldwide acceleration.**

## Problem
Travelers juggle SIM cards and pay zone fees. AT&T roaming stacks up at $12 a day, or $16 a day on boats. Your number and your assistant live in separate places.

## Value
$200M+ a year in new revenue from number subscriptions and worldwide calling.

## Solution
Grok Bot gets a virtual phone number, or you port in your existing one. It is anchored to a US home state (Texas, North Dakota or Florida) and works worldwide over the Starlink network. One number, one assistant, everywhere.

## How it grows the user base
Every traveler, sailor, deployed service member and remote worker is a customer. Each call introduces Grok Bot to the person on the other end.

## Fit with Musk/xAI direction
Ties Grok to Starlink: a worldwide network meets a worldwide assistant. It speeds up everything else on this list.

## Details
- No SIM swaps, no zone fees, no stacked roaming charges.
- Number porting keeps your existing contacts working.
- US home-state anchoring (Texas, North Dakota or Florida) keeps a stable legal and billing base.
