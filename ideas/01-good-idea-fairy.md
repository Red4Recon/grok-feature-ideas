# 1. Good Idea Fairy

**Bottom line: New revenue, $50M+ a year (Mike's estimate).**

## Problem
Good ideas die before anyone hears them. At USAA, more than 20,000 ideas a year swamped two people and burned them out. Long forms kill participation. Nobody checks patents or secrecy rules, and nobody gets rewarded.

## Value
$50M+ a year in new revenue from enterprise idea-pipeline subscriptions, faster shipping, and fewer failed projects. It also protects intellectual property and finds talent.

## Solution
Speak a raw idea. Grok turns it into a one-page pitch: problem, solution, value. It searches the patent office (USPTO) and flags anything that may fall under the Invention Secrecy Act. The idea then moves through stage gates with community voting, and strong ideas go to the Lightning Round.

## How it grows the user base
Every person with an idea becomes a user, and every company with an idea backlog becomes an enterprise customer. Shared pitches pull in voters and collaborators.

## Fit with Musk/xAI direction
Strips out bureaucracy: delete the form, keep the idea, let AI do the shaping. Same first-principles approach xAI uses to move fast.

## Details
- Modeled on USAA's ICE program, which Mike founded. USAA's mobile deposit came from a similar crowd-idea culture.
- Start with consumer voice capture plus the patent check. Then pilot with one company's idea pipeline. Target: 5x return in 18 months.
- Rewards and talent scouting: invite top contributors to co-develop, feed a hiring pipeline.
- Proof of approach: US11526165B1 (remote vehicle control).
