# 4. Clockwork Coach

**Bottom line: Cost savings plus retention. $64B a year in Military Health System (MHS) spend is addressable (Mike's figure). No revenue figure is claimed yet.**

## Problem
The Military Health System spends $64B a year. About 9.4 veterans die by suicide every day, and many had no VA contact in their final year. Today's AI only answers when you ask, and it forgets you when you hang up.

## Value
Cost savings and retention against a $64B yearly spend. Even small gains matter: by Mike's earlier math, a 1% efficiency gain would be about $640M a year, before counting lives saved.

## Solution
A coach that reaches out first: daily calls or texts at set times. It reads real health data from wearables (Apple Health, Whoop, Oura) and adjusts your plan, tracks streaks and shows projected healthy years. For units and care teams, it shows readiness and fatigue risk and flags suicide risk early from conversation and health signals.

## How it grows the user base
Starts with veterans and service members, then families, then everyone with a wearable. A daily check-in means daily use.

## Fit with Musk/xAI direction
Proactive AI that helps people live longer and better, built on real-world data. Competitors (ChatGPT Health) are already moving here.

## Details
- Consumer version first (Apple Health + Whoop). Then a pilot with one DoD unit or VA group. Target: 5x return in 18 months.
- Earlier package also cited: NFL injury spend $540M a year; a 10% cut is $54M.
- Includes the floating voice window (Picture-in-Picture) so coaching never takes over your screen.
