# 2. Lightning Round

**Bottom line: Cost savings, $20M+ a year (Mike's estimate).**

## Problem
Pitch meetings are slow and expensive. Ideas get judged on slides, not working demos, and only a few people ever see them.

## Value
$20M+ a year in cost savings: fewer meetings, faster yes/no decisions, and less money spent on projects that should have been stopped early.

## Solution
A set format for shaping ideas: a three-minute timed pitch with live screen share, a working demo, live comments and a scorecard. Grok coaches the pitch beforehand. On top of that, an automated weekly or monthly virtual Shark Tank, hosted with Grok and livestreamed so anyone in the world can watch.

## How it grows the user base
A recurring show on X brings a worldwide audience every week. Viewers become pitchers, and pitchers bring their own followers.

## Fit with Musk/xAI direction
Live, public and on X. It turns Grok into a stage, not just a tool, and puts real demos ahead of slide decks.

## Details
- Business sponsors can back winning ideas.
- Public dial-in on X, live comments and a results scoreboard.
- Feeds from, and back into, the Good Idea Fairy pipeline.
