# 3. Clone Me

**Bottom line: Premium tier plus retention, $100M+ a year (Mike's estimate).**

## Problem
Digital avatars fall flat. They don't have enough of your data to sound like you, so family and friends don't trust them.

## Value
$100M+ a year from a premium paid tier, plus users who stay for years because their clone keeps getting better.

## Solution
With your permission, Grok learns from your voice, conversations and decisions over months. The result is a lasting model of how you think and speak. Family and people you trust can talk to it. Generic, low-data avatars go away.

## How it grows the user base
Each clone is used by the owner's family and circle, so one user brings several more. Long capture periods make it hard to leave.

## Fit with Musk/xAI direction
Builds deeply personal AI on real data. Pairs with trust.me so the clone has legal standing.

## Details
- Opt-in consent toggle, then a 12-month capture pilot.
- Possible legal link in places that recognize AI entities (for example, the UAE).
- Uses also include family legacy and trust administration.
