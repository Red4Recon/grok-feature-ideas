# 6. trust.me

**Bottom line: New revenue plus retention, $150M+ a year (Mike's earlier estimate).**

## Problem
Your digital self (your data, voice, decisions and clone) has no legal home. When you die, it is stuck or lost. Banks offer trusts for money, but nobody offers one for your digital life.

## Value
$150M+ a year in new revenue from trust services, plus users who stay for life and beyond.

## Solution
A legal wrapper around your digital self. Post-death trust services like banks provide, lasting past your lifetime. Your wishes and choices kept as a library, beyond biology.

## How it grows the user base
Families and heirs join to reach the trust. Estate planners and advisors can refer clients.

## Fit with Musk/xAI direction
Long-term thinking about people and AI. Pairs with Clone Me to give the digital you real standing.

## Details
- Open question for xAI's legal team: what legal standing can a digital self have, and where?
- Looks at places that recognize AI entities (for example, the UAE).
- Modeled on bank trust and estate services.
